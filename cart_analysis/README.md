## Report Objectives
**business question** whether to increase the price increase of sweet snacks to the same level as other snacks and discretionary grocery.
*Establish elasticity* as prices increase for a particular deparment, does this impact shopper's willigness to visit the store? Should a shopper become disillusioned by the price of a product, will they shop less?
How does the presence of an item in a cart (a shopper's motivation to buy an item) impact the value of the cart?

KPIs by Department
  - proportion of carts by week (if cart contains at least 1 item in a Deparment)
  - avg value of carts by week if cart contains at least 1 item in a Department
  - repertoire: # of other departments present if a department is present in cart
